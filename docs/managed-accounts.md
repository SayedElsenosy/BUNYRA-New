# BUNYRA managed accounts
- Owner portal: /owner/login → /owner. Company portal: /company/login → /company. The server verifies the account type and company membership.
- The platform owner creates companies and their first administrator atomically, or selects an existing company and creates its accounts. Company administrators create employees within the active account limit.
- Only the platform owner changes company account limits. Active administrators count; disabled accounts free a seat. Creation and reactivation serialize on the company row, including concurrent requests.
- There are no invitations, external company signups, email verification or first-login password-change screens. New and reset passwords work immediately.
- Ordinary employees cannot edit their own name, username, contact email, password or other account details. The /account page redirects company users on the server before rendering. Its GET/POST API and underlying mutation are owner-only.
- Company administrators and Operations manage basic account details through the company Accounts & Permissions panel. The accounts.manage permission is granted to the built-in Operations role and can be explicitly denied or granted using company permissions/custom roles.
- Operations can edit employee details and reset employee passwords only in their own company. It cannot assign roles or raise account limits. Resetting an administrator password remains restricted to a company administrator or the platform owner to prevent gaining administrator authority through password reset.
- Basic detail edits can keep the existing password by leaving the new-password field empty. The editor updates the membership contact label and revokes existing sessions. Role/activation changes retain their existing members.manage authorization and last-admin protections.
- Passwords use native scrypt (N=16384,r=8,p=5), random per-account salts, and server-only hashes. Sessions use 256 random bits, database hashes, password-version checks, 12-hour expiry and a host-only HttpOnly SameSite=Lax cookie (Secure in production).
- Membership disabling and company suspension revoke sessions. Reactivation does not restore old tokens. Company purge removes its credentials and sessions while preserving the platform owner and audit trail.
- Login throttling persists per normalized username (10 attempts / 15 minutes) and verified Cloudflare source IP (60 attempts / 15 minutes).
- Existing verified owner identity can activate internal credentials while retaining its platform_owner user ID. Subsequent local sign-ins require no external IdP. Company provider sessions cannot bypass internal provisioning.
- Apply migrations 013-managed-accounts and 014-admin-managed-profiles before the new build. The latter clears any old forced-password-change flags and grants the new permission key in the database constraint.
- Credential/session tables enforce RLS and grant nothing to the tenant database role. Server credential access follows account-management authorization; responses and audit records never contain hashes or passwords.
- Verify with npm run typecheck and npm run test:accounts on the dedicated test database. Test fixtures refuse a real owner. On a future custom domain the same account URLs work; host-only cookies require a fresh sign-in.
